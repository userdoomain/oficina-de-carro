Frequently asked questions

How long does a diagnostic take?

The computerized diagnostic takes about 30 to 60 minutes on average. You receive the report with photos and the itemized quote during the same visit.

Do you work with original parts?

We work with original, genuine, and certified parts. The source is stated in the quote, and you choose the option that best suits your budget and needs.

What does the 12-month warranty cover?

All labor and parts replaced by us. If the service shows any problem within that period, the follow-up and repair are 100% free.

Do you service imported cars?

Yes. Our team is trained for both domestic and imported vehicles, with scanners compatible with the leading brands on the market.

Do I need to leave my car overnight?

In most cases, no. Checkups and oil changes are completed the same day. More complex repairs usually take 1 to 3 business days.
