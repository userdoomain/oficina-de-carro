Frequently asked questions

How long does a diagnostic take?

A computer diagnostic takes around 30–60 minutes. You'll get a report with photos and an itemised quote during the same visit.

Do you use genuine parts?

We use genuine, original and certified parts. The provenance is shown on the quote — you choose what suits your budget and needs.

What does the 12-month guarantee cover?

All labour and parts we replace. If anything goes wrong during that period, the return visit and fix are completely free.

Do you service imported cars?

Yes. Our team is trained on both domestic and imported vehicles, with scanners compatible with all major brands.

Do I need to leave the car overnight?

Usually not. Servicing and oil changes are done the same day. More complex repairs typically take 1–3 business days.
